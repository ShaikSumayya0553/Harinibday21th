## 🌿 Aesthetic Features

- **Sage Green Gingham Background**: Checkered fabric texture with green ribbon bow accents and white tulip corner bouquets.
- **Ambient Particles**: Floating 4-leaf clovers (🍀), white tulips (🌷), green butterflies (🦋), green bows (🎀), and green hearts (💚).
- **Virtual Hug Final Screen**: Two cute baby calves hugging tightly with blushing cheeks and wrapping arms, floating clovers, and the required texts:
  - `"A Virtual Hug For You!"`
  - `"I MISS YOU ❤️"`
- **Replay Our Memories**: Fully interactive, tracks all 5 letters and allows revisiting anytime!
